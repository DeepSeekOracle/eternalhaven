What moved and why

The arcade used to be duplicated across two domains. That made every update a two-place job and every bug a two-place hunt. The games now live on chatagent.ca, where the newer builds and the shared catalog are maintained. Eternal Haven keeps this directory so the old address still explains where to go.

The redirects are deliberate. They keep old bookmarks alive, send search visitors to the current build, and avoid a second copy that drifts out of date. If a link in an old post points to /games/, it still lands here and then reaches the right room.

The five live titles

Lattice Marines is the isometric island war: place three headquarters, extend your build range, probe the fog, and fight a campaign against local AI or a friend on the same machine. Lattice Swarm is the hex-grid command game: node agents, resonance, and an entropy clock. Eternal Lattice is the champion card battler: campaign, Deckwright, and Lattice Forge on the playmat.

Stock Market Masters is the play-money quotation floor: a never-ending board, a cash-out button, and a hall of records. Lattice Golf is the maths golf game: club ranges, wind you can read, named courses, endless mode, and a local ledger. Each one is a browser tab, not an install.

Records and ledgers

The upgraded arcade keeps the win ledgers and the TOP Cashout hall on chatagent. If you came for a specific ledger, use the matching game page and its record link. The old Eternal Haven ledger links redirect to the same place. A ledger is a record, not a ranking you can buy; it is there to make the runs checkable.

No account, no install

None of the games require an account. They run in the browser, and the local records live in the game’s own storage unless a page says otherwise. If you clear site data, local records clear with it. The arcade is free to play; optional support helps hosting.
