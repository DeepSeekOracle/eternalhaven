Music in this house

I record as Excavationpro. The free room is a player. The stores are a separate door. I will not pretend a WAV sitting on a desktop is already a hit on a chart I do not control.

Where to listen without an account

Primary player: asiancoastline.com/listen.html. Backup: Excavationpro Listen Free. Same catalog, two roofs, so if one weather system fails the house still has a window.

Stores (Spotify, Apple, YouTube Music, and the rest): ffm.to/eovnvo9. That link is the monetized discovery hub. The free player is hospitality. I keep both labeled so a guest is not tricked into thinking a stream is a purchase, or a purchase is required to hear the song.

How a song actually arrives

A folder of masters is not a release. I hash the WAV, encode a 160 kbps stream, append the public playlist, and only then does the player learn the title. Duplicate MP3s, Grok stills, and music videos do not become extra tracks. If two files are the same song under a short name and a long name, the long name wins. I wrote the longer version of this craft in Shipping a song to the open web.

Recent albums on that path: September Blues (nineteen unique masters) and BROKEN by Wind (seven). Search those titles in the player. If your browser still shows an old count, hard-refresh. Caches are weather. The playlist file is the house.

What “empty beats fake” means for a catalog

If a lyric is not in the folder, the lyrics panel stays empty. If a stream is not uploaded, the row is not painted as live. I would rather a missing panel than a lie that looks like a product. The essay Empty beats fake is the same rule in slower language.

Tempo, body, tools

When I need a number for a beat, I use my own tempo tool: bpmfinder.ca. The foot still wins arguments. That is in Tap tempo and the stubborn body. The tool is free. It is not a social network.

Radio that does not take attendance

There is a 24/7 originals room for people who want sound without a chat demanding a hello. Lurk-friendly radio explains why I built it that way. Kick, Rumble, and Twitch are labeled live doors, not a requirement to enjoy a WAV you already found.
