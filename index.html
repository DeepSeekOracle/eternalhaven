A house, not a kiosk

Feeds are weather. Sites are houses. I would rather own a small messy hallway than rent a clean cage. This homepage is for sitting: a few honest doors, writing that could only have come from this desk, and no requirement that you perform a like before you lurk. The dense systems map still exists — the hub — one click away for people who already live here. Guests get a chair first.

I am Justin Helmer. I also publish music as Excavationpro. Steward work sometimes uses the name Lightfather. You do not need those names to read a chapter. You can learn them on the about page if you want a second tour.
