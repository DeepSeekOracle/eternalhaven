Listen to the catalog

No account, no preview wall, no countdown. The player lives on another domain because audio is heavy; this page tells you which door to use and what the deal is.

The music lives on a different domain because a catalog of original audio is heavy, and this domain is a library with a small server bill. That is the whole explanation. There is no strategy in it, and nothing is being withheld here.

The main door

asiancoastline.com/listen.html is the browser player. Search, shuffle, album play, a queue, and a radio mode that keeps going without asking you to choose. No login. It works on a phone. It does not autoplay at you from a page you did not ask to play.

The backup door

The GitHub Pages listen hub is the second door, useful when a host is having a bad day or when you want a page that loads with almost nothing on it. Same catalog family, plainer room.

Stores exist too: Spotify, YouTube Music, and Audius carry a subset. They are not the primary door, because a store can change its terms and a domain you own cannot be delisted by a policy update. If you already live in one of those apps, use it; the same recordings are there.

What "free to listen" covers

You may listen. You may download copies offered for personal listening. You may share an official link. You may not sell the tracks, use them as a beat pack, put them under a monetised video, or feed them to a model you intend to resell. The full wording is the music license, which is short and was written to be read rather than survived.

The distinction that matters is between access and ownership. Free listening is a gift of access. It is not a transfer of rights, and it never was. If you need a commercial sync, ask: contact reaches me, and I answer those messages.

How a song arrives here

The long version is Music in this house. The short version: a track is mixed, mastered, encoded to a stream, given an identity, hashed, published, and then linked from a page that explains it. The hash is not decoration. It means the file you hear is the file that was published, which matters more for a small catalog than for a large one, because a small catalog has no legal department to argue for it.

Live rooms

When there is a live room, the links are on excavationpro.ca and in the hub. I do not embed a player that starts by itself on this page, because a page about listening should not make noise before you choose to listen.
